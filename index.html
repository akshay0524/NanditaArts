Meet the Artist

Nandita Albright

Nandita Albright is a self-taught artist whose work draws from the raw energy of the Abstract Expressionists and the meditative traditions of South Asian art. Based in the United States, she has been creating original paintings for over two decades.

Her paintings inhabit a space where structure dissolves into gesture — where chaos and calm coexist on a single canvas. Working primarily in acrylic and oil, Nandita builds up rich, textured surfaces with palette knives and brushes, creating layers that invite the viewer to look deeper.

Each piece in her collection is an original, one-of-a-kind work, signed and shipped directly from her studio to your home.
