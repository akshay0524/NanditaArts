"Crimson Reverie" is a powerfully expressive work born from a period of intense creative energy. Nandita built up multiple layers of rich crimson, burnt orange, and gold leaf using palette knives and wide brushes, creating a surface that catches light in constantly shifting ways.

No two viewings of this painting are the same — the texture creates shadows and highlights that move with the time of day. This is an original, one-of-a-kind piece.
